I am a Computer Scientist in the Future Computing Technologies Group group at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory ([PNNL](https://www.pnnl.gov/)) in [Richland, WA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richland,_Washington). I have a broad range of research interests in several aspects of HPC, such as, performance characterization & analysis, proxy applications driven co-design, graph analytics and combinatorial optimizations, application of standardized parallel programming models (such as MPI and OpenMP) for building application kernels and development of one-sided/PGAS communication abstractions. I am a Senior Member of IEEE (# 92684851).

I hold a Masters degree from University of Houston in Houston, TX and a Ph.D. degree (both in Computer Sciences) from Washington State University in Pullman, WA.
